Notice of Collection
Personal information on this form is collected under the authority of the City of Toronto Act, 2006, the Municipal Act, 2001, the City of Toronto Municipal Code and other applicable legislation. The information will be used to process the requested change(s) for the affected properties, mailing required bills and notices, and for contacting the owner/authorized agent if clarification is required. Questions about this collection can be directed to Revenue Services, 5100 Yonge St, Toronto ON M2N 5V7 or by phone at 416-395-1048.
