Notice of Collection
Personal information on this form is collected under the authority of City of Toronto Act, S.O. 2006, Chapter 11, Schedule A, s.136 (c), 261 and 264, Ontario Regulation 594/06 (Fees and Charges–Priority Lien Status) and the City of Toronto Municipal Code, Chapter 849 (Water and Sewage Services and Utility Bill). The information will be used for proper administration of taxes, fees, and charges that are imposed by the City for the supply of services, which will include but is not limited to the updating of ownership/mailing information concerning the tax/utility bill account(s) for the affected properties, mailing required bills and notices and collection of unpaid amounts. The information may also be used for purposes of identifying agents of owners for purposes of authorizing changes to Tax/Utility accounts on behalf of the owner. Questions about this collection can be directed to the Manager, Customer Service, Revenue Services, 5100 Yonge St, Toronto ON M2N 5V7 or by phone at 416-395-1048.
